Privacy Policy for Peitho

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Peitho is a daily impromptu-speaking practice app. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how you can control it.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up with email, we collect your email address and a password to create and secure your account. Your password is never stored in plain text. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and basic profile information (such as your name) from Google; we never see your Google password.
  • Voice recordings. When you record a practice speech, the app captures audio through your device's microphone so you can play it back and review it later. Recordings are uploaded and stored securely so you can access your practice history from your account.
  • Transcripts. After a recording, a transcript is created so you can read your speech alongside the audio. By default this uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (Apple's or Google's). Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio on your device or send it to Apple's or Google's servers; its handling is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours. You can optionally switch to ElevenLabs transcription (see below).
  • Feedback. If you ask for feedback, the app scores your talk and writes short coaching notes: scores for skills such as clarity and flow, what went well, and what to try next time. These are saved with the talk so you don't have to generate them again. The coaching model runs on your device; your transcript is not sent anywhere to produce it.
  • Practice activity. We keep a record of your sessions (such as prompts practiced, dates, and streaks) so the app can show your progress over time.

Optional: ElevenLabs transcription

In Settings you can choose ElevenLabs for the transcription used in feedback. It is off by default. If you turn it on and ask for feedback on a talk, that talk's audio recording is sent, through our server, to ElevenLabs, which returns a transcript with word timings so we can measure filler words and pauses. We store the returned transcript with your talk so the audio is sent at most once per talk. ElevenLabs handles the audio under its own privacy policy. If you leave the setting on Device, no audio is sent to ElevenLabs.

What we don't collect

  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We do not access your microphone unless you actively start a recording.

How your data is used

Your recordings, transcripts, feedback, and practice history are used only to run the core features of the app: letting you record, review, and track your own speaking practice. We don't use your recordings to train any models, and we don't share them with other users. The only other parties that handle your data are the service providers described on this page.

Where your data is stored and who handles it

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in. Your account data, recordings, transcripts, and feedback are stored there. Supabase acts strictly as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes.
  • ElevenLabs, only if you choose it, as described above.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Hugging Face hosts the coaching model file. If you use it, the app downloads the model once to your device (about 2 GB). Nothing from your talks is sent when it does.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. It does not send any information to us or anyone else.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete individual talks, including their recordings, from within the app at any time.
  • You can delete your entire account from the app: open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your talks, recordings, transcripts, and feedback. It cannot be undone. You can also ask us to do this by emailing the address below.
  • You can revoke microphone or speech-recognition permission at any time from your device settings, though this will prevent recording and transcription from working.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. When you do, it is removed from our database and storage.

Children's privacy

Peitho is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
