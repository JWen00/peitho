Privacy Policy for Peitho

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Peitho is a daily impromptu-speaking practice app. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and password to create and secure your account. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • Voice recordings. When you record a practice speech, the app captures audio through your device's microphone so you can play it back and review it later. Recordings are uploaded and stored securely so you can access your practice history from your account.
  • Transcripts. After a recording, the app uses on-device or OS-level speech recognition to transcribe what you said, so you can read back your speech alongside the audio.
  • Practice activity. We keep a record of your sessions (such as prompts practiced, dates, and streaks) so the app can show your progress over time.

What we don't collect

  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We do not access your microphone unless you actively start a recording.

How your data is used

Your recordings, transcripts, and practice history are used only to run the core features of the app — letting you record, review, and track your own speaking practice. We don't use your recordings to train any models, and we don't share them with other users or outside parties.

Where your data is stored

Your account data, recordings, and transcripts are stored with Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, which hosts our database and file storage. Supabase acts strictly as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete individual recordings from within the app at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your entire account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We'll delete it within a reasonable time.
  • You can revoke microphone or speech-recognition permission at any time from your device settings, though this will prevent recording and transcription from working.

Children's privacy

Peitho is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
